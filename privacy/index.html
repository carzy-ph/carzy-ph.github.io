Carzy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Carzy gives car sales agents a digital business card (opened by tapping an NFC card or a link) where clients can contact the agent and apply for a car loan. This policy explains what information Carzy collects, why, and how it is protected. Carzy follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines.

Information we collect

  • Agent profiles: name, title, dealership, branch, contact numbers, email, photos and social links. These appear publicly on the agent’s card while the card is active.
  • Portal sign-in: when agents and admins sign in with Google, we receive their name and email address to confirm they are on the team.
  • Car loan applications: the unit applied for; the applicant’s name, birthday, birthplace, mother’s maiden name, civil status, contact details, address, employment, income and bank; and the same details for any co-makers.
  • Requirements: documents a client chooses to upload (such as valid IDs and proof of income).
  • Basic usage: when a card is opened and how (NFC tap, QR code or link), without identifying the visitor.

How we use it

  • To show agents’ public cards and let clients contact them.
  • To deliver loan applications to the agent the client chose, so the agent can follow up.
  • To evaluate the application: with the client’s consent, the agent and dealership may share the application with partner banks and financing companies for credit evaluation.
  • To keep the service secure, for example by limiting repeated submissions.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Google user data

  • Google sign-in (agents and admins): Carzy receives your name and email address only, to confirm you can access the portal.
  • Google Drive (agents who choose “Connect Google Drive”): Carzy requests the drive.file permission, which only allows it to create and manage the files and folders it makes. Carzy creates a “Carzy requirements” folder in your Drive and saves documents your clients upload there. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.
  • Carzy stores the connection securely on its server so uploads keep working. Only Carzy’s upload service can use it; it is never shown on the website. You can disconnect at any time from your portal, or remove Carzy at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already saved stay in your Drive.
  • Carzy’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see applications

Only the agent the client applied through and the dealership’s Carzy administrators can view an application. Uploaded documents are stored in the agent’s own Google Drive. Agents and administrators are responsible for handling this information only for the client’s loan application.

Where data is stored

Application data and agent profiles are stored with our database provider (Supabase) over encrypted connections. Agent photos are stored there as well. Uploaded requirements are stored in the agent’s Google Drive.

How long we keep it

Applications are kept as long as needed to process the loan and for the dealership’s records, then deleted on request or when no longer needed. When an agent’s card is turned off, their profile is no longer shown publicly.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent. Contact the agent you applied through, or the Carzy administrator, and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
